    FAQ

    The questions travellers ask us before they book.

    When do I see the price?+

    As soon as you choose your dates and press Check live price. Every price is checked live when you ask for it, so it reflects your dates, rooms and guests.

    How long can I stay?+

    Up to 30 nights in one booking. For a longer journey, book the stays one after another in Build your trip.

    Can each room have different guests?+

    In one search every room has the same guests: 1 to 9 rooms, 1 to 20 adults and 0 to 20 children per room. If your rooms differ, search them separately and add both to your trip.

    How do I pay?+

    Payments are made through DOKU or SNAP BI at checkout.

    Can I combine a hotel with a tour?+

    Yes. Add stays and activities to Build your trip and pay for them together.
