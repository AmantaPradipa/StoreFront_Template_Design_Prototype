Frequently Asked Questions

Key details on reservations, multi-room group stays, and payment processing.

How does live price checking work? +

When you enter travel dates and guest numbers, our S4 engine checks real-time availability and dynamic retail rates. Every price is checked live when you ask for it.

What are the multi-room and occupancy limits? +

You can book 1 to 9 rooms in a single transaction for stays up to 30 nights. Each room supports 1 to 20 adults and 0 to 20 children, with uniform guest distribution across all selected rooms.

Which payment methods are accepted? +

We support verified Indonesian bank transfers, QRIS, and virtual accounts via SNAP BI and DOKU checkout rails.

Can I bundle resort stays with private boat transfers? +

Yes! Use the "Build your trip" drawer to combine any resort stay with boat charters or guided hikes, receiving a consolidated itinerary and single checkout voucher.
