# Research — 05 Rimba Trek Adventures (Adventure & Nature)

## Visual & UX Conventions
1. **Field-Station Dispatch Architecture**: Outdoor expedition operators (NOLS, REI Adventures, Patagonia Provisions) frame trip discovery as technical route dossiers rather than leisure brochures.
2. **Telemetry & Topographic Clarity**: Elevation (MDPL), route grade, duration, and trekker-to-guide ratios must be scannable in a top spec bar using condensed display typography (`Barlow Condensed`).
3. **Sticky Vertical Dispatch Console**: A 4-column left dispatch console paired with an 8-column route catalog enables rapid sector and window filtering.
4. **Trail-Orange High-Visibility CTA**: Trail orange (`#D97736`) requires dark forest ink (`#0C241D`, 7.31:1 contrast) rather than white text (2.82:1) to meet WCAG 2.1 AA.
5. **Safety & Preparation Matrix**: Explicit pre-trip gear, acclimatization, and zero-waste protocols build trust without inventing unverified certification claims.
