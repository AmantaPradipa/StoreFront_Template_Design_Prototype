Bagian 01 · Strategi & Riset Segmen

Posisi Merek & Temuan Riset Desain (Adventure & Nature)

01

Field-Station Dispatch Architecture: Outdoor expedition operators (NOLS, REI Adventures, Patagonia Provisions) frame trip discovery as technical route dossiers rather than leisure brochures.

02

Telemetry & Topographic Clarity: Elevation (MDPL), route grade, duration, and trekker-to-guide ratios must be scannable in a top spec bar using condensed display typography (`Barlow Condensed`).

03

Sticky Vertical Dispatch Console: A 4-column left dispatch console paired with an 8-column route catalog enables rapid sector and window filtering.

04

Trail-Orange High-Visibility CTA: Trail orange (`#D97736`) requires dark forest ink (`#0C241D`, 7.31:1 contrast) rather than white text (2.82:1) to meet WCAG 2.1 AA.

05

Safety & Preparation Matrix: Explicit pre-trip gear, acclimatization, and zero-waste protocols build trust without inventing unverified certification claims.

Bagian 06 & 07 · Unique Architectural & Component DNA

Pola Tata Letak & Komponen Khas Preset 05

Hero Architecture (Tactical Expedition Telemetry Banner):

A high-contrast forest-pine banner (`#183B32`) with a bottom telemetry ticker bar displaying elevation peaks (`3.726 MDPL`), APGI guide certification status, and national park Simaksi live sync.

Main Workspace Layout (2-Column Sticky Field-Station Split):

Instead of a full-width top search bar over a 3-column grid, the main body is a 12-column split workspace: the **Left 4 Columns** house a sticky vertical **Field-Station Dispatch Search Console** (`lg:sticky lg:top-24`), and the **Right 8 Columns** display the live expedition & basecamp catalog.

Search Engine UI (Vertical Field-Station Dispatch Console):

Stacked vertical tactical controls with high-contrast condensed labels (`SECTOR / CONSERVATION ZONE`, `EXPEDITION WINDOW`, `SQUAD & TENT MATRIX`) and a full-width trail-orange (`#D97736`) quota check trigger.

Inventory Section Layout (2-Column Topographic Dossier Cards):

Cards feature a top technical spec strip (`ELEVATION / DURATION / GRADE`), bold condensed titles, gear/logistics tags, and an orange-accented bottom quota bar.

Safety & Preparation Section (Expedition Gear & Protocol Matrix):

A 4-column tactical checklist grid detailing mandatory APGI safety gear, Simaksi permit sync, zero-waste mountaineering, and DOKU/SNAP BI settlement.

Bagian 09 · Praktik Wajib (Do’s)

  • ✓ Do show difficulty, duration, or requirements only when verified.

Bagian 10 · Larangan (Don’ts & Invariants)

Batasan Kontrak Produk Ortasys S4

  • ✕ Don't use danger aesthetics, camouflage, distressed textures, or aggressive motion as shortcuts for adventure.
  • ✕ Don't invent safety guarantees, certifications, environmental claims, or guide credentials.
  • ✕ Don't imply activities are bookable if inventory is unavailable.
  • ✕ Don't let large hero imagery push search too far down the page.
  • ✕ Don't expose supplier net rates, agent margins, or internal B2B information.
