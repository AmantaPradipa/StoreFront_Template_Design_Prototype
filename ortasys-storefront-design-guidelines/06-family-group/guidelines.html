Bagian 06 & 07 · Unique Architectural & Component DNA

Pola Tata Letak & Komponen Khas Preset 06

Hero Architecture (Warm Family Scrapbook Split):

A soft rounded container (`rounded-3xl`) where the left 6 columns present reassuring family headlines and verified comfort badges (`Connecting Rooms`, `Stroller-Friendly`), and the right 6 columns display a 2x2 photo scrapbook collage of multi-generational moments.

Search Engine UI (3-Step Guided Family Planner Wizard):

Instead of a raw form row, the search engine is divided into 3 numbered visual step-cards inside a soft cream container (`rounded-3xl`): **Langkah 1: Pilih Tujuan & Tanggal**, **Langkah 2: Atur Rombongan & Kamar (1–9 Unit)** with a live **"Total Anggota Rombongan: X Orang"** calculator pill, and **Langkah 3: Cek Kuota Kamar Terhubung**.

Inventory Section Layout (2-Column Wide Family Comparison Cards):

Wide 2-column horizontal/stacked family cards (`rounded-3xl`) featuring a dedicated **Matriks Kenyamanan Keluarga** table inside each card (Kapasitas Unit, Konfigurasi Kasur/Pintu, Fasilitas Anak/Lansia) plus a **"Estimasi Patungan per Orang"** helper readout alongside the total retail price.

Why Us Section (3-Step Horizontal Family Journey Timeline):

A visual step-by-step timeline (`1. Pilih Vila Terhubung` $\rightarrow$ `2. Satu Invoice Rombongan SNAP BI` $\rightarrow$ `3. Sambutan Ramah Anak & Lansia`).

Bagian 09 · Praktik Wajib (Do’s)

Pedoman Implementasi Positif

  • ✓ Do make room and guest configuration easy to understand.
  • ✓ Do use welcoming but credible language.
  • ✓ Do surface verified family-relevant property information.
  • ✓ Do keep group enquiry distinct from instant booking when workflows differ.
Bagian 10 · Larangan (Don’ts & Invariants)

Batasan Kontrak Produk Ortasys S4

  • ✕ Don't use childish illustrations or cartoon visual language.
  • ✕ Don't promise connecting rooms, child facilities, or discounts without evidence.
  • ✕ Don't invent group booking functionality.
  • ✕ Don't hide occupancy rules in fine print.
  • ✕ Don't expose supplier net rates or agent-only data.
