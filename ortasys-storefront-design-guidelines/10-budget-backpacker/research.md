# Research — 10 Jalan Hemat Travel (Budget & Backpacker)

## Visual & UX Conventions
1. **Transit Departure-Board Split**: Budget & hostel platforms convert best when real-time base rates and route tickers appear in the first viewport.
2. **Perforated Ticket-Stub Console**: Framing search and inventory cards as physical perforated boarding passes with a tear-off per-person split calculator builds price trust.
3. **Free-Inclusion Transparency**: Backpackers scan for value-saving inclusions (`FREE LOCKER 60L`, `FREE REFILL WATER`, `NO CURFEW`) alongside bed type (`CAPSULE POD`, `BUNK DORM`).
4. **Coral CTA with High-Contrast Dark Ink**: Coral `#E56B45` uses dark ink `#192C45` to exceed 4.5:1 AA contrast.
